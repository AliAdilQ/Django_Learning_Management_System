Luma is a complete Django Learning Management System, created by AliAdilQ as a portfolio project.

A working platform, from first
lesson to final feedback.

This project brings together the everyday parts of an online classroom: discovery, course delivery, progress, assignments, quizzes, and instructor tools.

Students can follow a curriculum, save lesson progress, submit private assignments, and review courses. Instructors build their own courses and give feedback. Administrators manage the platform through Django's configured admin interface.

Built with Python, Django, SQLite, Bootstrap 5, and vanilla JavaScript, the project focuses on clear code, accessible interfaces, and careful permissions. The fictional brand, sample courses, accounts, reviews, and activity are for demonstration. Luma is not a commercial learning provider, and its sample lessons are not accredited courses.
